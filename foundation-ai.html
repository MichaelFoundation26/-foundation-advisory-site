The Challenge

AI adoption is moving faster than most organizations’ policies and controls.

Employees are already using public AI tools to draft content, summarize documents, analyze data, and make decisions. At the same time, vendors are embedding AI into products and platforms without always making the related risks clear.

Without practical governance, organizations can unintentionally expose confidential information, rely on inaccurate output, use unapproved tools, create inconsistent practices, or make decisions without appropriate human review.

Foundation AI gives leadership a clear, right-sized way to understand current use, establish guardrails, and move forward responsibly.

Common Questions We Help Answer

  • Which AI tools are employees already using?
  • What information should never be entered into public AI systems?
  • Which tools should be approved, restricted, or prohibited?
  • Who is accountable for reviewing AI risk and adoption?
  • How should AI-generated work be reviewed and validated?
  • What should leadership prioritize next?
What Foundation AI Provides

A practical foundation for responsible AI use.

Our services are designed to help organizations move from informal experimentation to clear, documented, and business-aligned AI adoption.

1

Readiness & Risk Review

Understand current AI use, business objectives, information risks, leadership concerns, and the gaps that should be addressed first.

2

Policies & Employee Guidance

Establish clear expectations for acceptable use, prohibited data, human review, accountability, and responsible use across the organization.

3

Approved Tool Standards

Define which AI tools are approved, conditionally approved, restricted, or prohibited based on security, privacy, business, and contractual risk.

4

Vendor & Platform Review

Evaluate AI-enabled vendors and platforms with practical questions about data use, retention, access, model training, and contractual terms.

5

Executive Roadmap

Give leadership a prioritized path for governance, security, employee enablement, platform decisions, and future AI initiatives.

6

Ongoing Advisory

Maintain momentum through policy updates, vendor evaluations, executive guidance, governance reviews, and support for new use cases.
